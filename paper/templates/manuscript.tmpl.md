## Abstract

### Background
`VarDict` (`VarDictJava` v1.8.3) is a heuristic-aware local realigner and somatic/germline variant caller widely used in clinical oncology and circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) minimal residual disease (MRD) pipelines. Streaming alignments through a Java pileup engine piped to external R and Perl scripts causes unbounded per-interval memory growth and runtime crashes on deep sequencing, while `vardictcpp` omits splicing, amplicon VCF, structural variant, and CRAM modes.

### Results
We present `VarDictRust` (`v{{m:current_release_version}}`; `{{m:total_cells}}`-experiment cloud study at `v{{m:version}}`), a single-binary, `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` Rust consolidation built under [rewrites.bio](https://rewrites.bio). Replicating R's 5-decimal `HALF_EVEN` Fisher exact test, default `--compat=vardictjava` matches `{{m:oracle_version}}` (`ε = {{m:eps_tol}}`) across `{{m:s2_modes_rust_match}}/{{m:s2_modes_total}}` operational modes (`95%` Clopper-Pearson confidence interval [CI] `{{m:cp95_13_13}}`), `{{m:s4_ext_cov_modalities}}` modalities (`95%` CI `{{m:cp95_17_17}}`), `{{m:s4_multimodal_slices}}` slices (`95%` CI `{{m:cp95_18_18}}`), `{{m:s5_f0_noise_floor_strict_match}}` `-f {{m:f_zero}}` noise-floor sweeps (`95%` CI `{{m:cp95_noise_18_18}}`), and all completing paired runs across `{{m:s5_cross_platform_arms}}` short-read chemistries (`{{m:s6_ga4gh_cells}}/{{m:s6_ga4gh_cells}}` GA4GH `v3.5` truth strata). On contiguous chromosome 1 (`{{m:s2_rchr1_mbp}}` Mbp), bounded `O(tile + halo)` streaming finishes in `{{m:s2_rchr1_rust_wall_s}}` s and `{{m:s2_rchr1_rust_rss_mb}}` MB resident memory (`{{m:s2_rchr1_speedup_vs_java}}`× faster and `{{m:s2_rchr1_rss_ratio_vs_java}}`× leaner than `VarDictJava` `-Xmx200g`; `{{m:s2_rchr1_speedup_vs_cpp}}`× faster and `{{m:s2_rchr1_rss_ratio_vs_cpp}}`× leaner than `{{m:cpp_version}}`). Within `128` GiB (`n2-standard-32`; `-Xmx200g` baselines on `256` GiB `n2-highmem-32`), `VarDictRust` completes unchunked `{{m:s5_hg002_bam_gb}}` GB `HG002` (`{{m:s5_hg002_contigs_rust_rss_gb}}` GB RAM) and `{{m:s5_hg008_bam_gb}}` GB `364`× `HG008` somatic WGS (`{{m:s5_hg008_contigs_rust_rss_gb}}` GB RAM), where `VarDictJava` runs out of memory and `vardictcpp` times out, gaining `+{{m:s6_v50q_snv_r_gain_pct}}` SNV and `+{{m:s6_v50q_indel_r_gain_pct}}` indel recall on `NIST GIAB v5.0q T2T-Q100` (`+{{m:s6_cmrg_snv_r_gain_pct}}` and `+{{m:s6_cmrg_indel_r_gain_pct}}` on `CMRG v1.00`). Opt-in `--compat=off` (`{{m:v110_compat_switch_count}}` switches) and `--preset` (`wgs`, `wes`, `amplicon`, `ctdna`) resolve all `{{m:v110_schema_defects_vj}}` targeted VCF schema/canonicalization findings across `{{m:v110_arm1_callsets}}` callsets (`{{m:v110_bcftools_norm_off}}` `bcftools norm` pass) and rescue `+{{m:v110_arm2_tp_gain}}/{{m:v110_arm2_truth_total}}` (`+{{m:v110_arm2_recall_gain_pp}}` pp) `GT=0/0`-masked `SEQC2`-slice somatic calls in ploidy-strict `vcfeval` (`+{{m:v110_arm2_f1_gain}}` optimal $F_1$).

### Conclusions
`VarDictRust` consolidates a three-language variant-calling pipeline (`{{m:s5_h4_conda_pkgs}}` Conda packages) into a `{{m:s5_h4_musl_mb}}` MiB static Linux binary with default oracle equivalence (`ε = {{m:eps_tol}}`), bounded streaming memory, and opt-in VCFv4.2/v4.3 clinical presets across targeted panels, RNA-seq splicing, unchunked deep WGS, and `{{m:s6_tit_vaf_1_pct}}` VAF assays.

## Background

Clinical oncology and liquid-biopsy assays must detect somatic SNVs, multinucleotide variants (MNVs), indels, and structural variants (SVs) across variable tumor purities and cell-free DNA (cfDNA) fractions down to `{{m:s6_tit_vaf_1_pct}}`–`{{m:s6_tit_vaf_2_pct}}` VAF [1, 2], whereas germline variant callers such as `FreeBayes` [3], `Strelka2` [4], `Octopus` [5], and `DeepVariant` [6] model diploid genotype likelihoods [7, 8]. Introduced by Lai et al. in 2016 [1], `VarDict` (`VarDictJava` v1.8.3) integrates local soft-clip and indel realignment (`CigarModifier` and `VariationRealigner`). It adds composite MNP phasing, amplicon primer-edge filtering (`-a`), RNA-seq splice-junction accounting (`-i`), and inline SV breakpoint assembly. `VarDict` is deployed across clinical panel pipelines, `bcbio-nextgen`, `nf-core/sarek`, and multi-caller somatic consensus ensembles [9, 10].

Reference `VarDictJava` v1.8.3 has four structural limits on deep WGS and cloud infrastructure:

1. **Three-Language Pipeline and IPC Overhead.** `VarDictJava` emits an intermediate `36`- to `61`-column tab-delimited `.var` stream. Column counts are `Simple` `36/38`, `Amplicon` `38/40`, and `Somatic` `55/61` without/with `--fisher`. That stream pipes to an R script (`teststrandbias.R` or `testsomatic.R`) for Fisher exact testing. It then pipes to a Perl script (`var2vcf_valid.pl` or `var2vcf_paired.pl`) for filtering and VCF 4.2 formatting (`Figure 1`). Even when `--fisher` runs inside Java, the Perl post-processor remains mandatory, requiring `{{m:s5_h4_conda_pkgs}}` Conda packages across OpenJDK, R (`statmod`), and Perl.

2. **Unbounded JVM Heap Retention on Contiguous Spans.** `VarDictJava` allocates per-position pileup maps (`Map<Integer, VariationMap<String, Variation>>` and reference base arrays) across the full BED interval or `-R` region. Variant finalization (`ToVarsBuilder`) and realignment (`VariationRealigner`) only run afterwards. On megabase or chromosome-scale intervals, RSS grows linearly with interval length (`O(L × depth)`). That causes `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError` crashes or garbage-collection stalls above `100`–`200` GiB RAM (`Table 2`, `Table 5`).

3. **Runtime Exceptions and Non-Determinism on Edge-Case Reads.** Over `30` upstream `VarDictJava` issues report `CigarModifier.modifyCigar` crashes. Affected issues include `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#7`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#38`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#187`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#197`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#243`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#249`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#253`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#282`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#283`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#320`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#341`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#380`, and `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#384`. The crashes hit supplementary or heavily soft-clipped reads with `NullPointerException`, `StringIndexOutOfBoundsException`, and `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException`. Other issues cover non-deterministic row ordering from unseeded `java.util.HashMap` iteration (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#282`), broken CRAM decoding (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#249`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#380`), and truncated `##contig` VCF headers (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#320`).

4. **Incomplete C++ Port (`vardictcpp`).** Wolff (2026) introduced `{{m:cpp_version}}`, a C++/htslib reimplementation targeting single-sample and paired somatic speedups [11]. Our 3-way audit identifies 12 functional and statistical divergences in `vardictcpp` (`Table 8`; `Supplementary Table S6`). Specifically, `vardictcpp` omits `##contig` and `INFO/END` VCF headers. It omits CRISPR (`-J`/`-j`), structural variant (`--nosv` forced by default), and RNA splice (`-i`) modes. It emits raw tab-separated text instead of VCF in amplicon (`-a`) mode. It rejects concatenated CLI flags. It diverges on CRAM decoding (`-G`), `b37` numeric contigs, and MNP phasing/tie-breaking. It consumes unbounded memory on large intervals (`{{m:s5_hg002_contigs_cpp_rss_gb}}` GB on HG002 WGS; `{{m:s5_hg008_contigs_cpp_rss_gb}}` GB with a `3`-hour timeout on HG008 WGS). Pre-chunked regions gain `+{{m:s5_hg002_contigs_cpp_excess}}` duplicate boundary calls (`--chunk {{m:cpp_chunk_bp}}`).

We built `VarDictRust` (`v{{m:current_release_version}}`; empirical cloud campaign executed at `v{{m:version}}`) under the [rewrites.bio](https://rewrites.bio) prime directive to consolidate the pipeline into a memory-safe, single-binary Rust implementation. Its default mode (`--compat=vardictjava` / `--parity`) is byte- and float-equivalent (`ε = {{m:eps_tol}}`) to `VarDictJava` v1.8.3 on all valid inputs, eliminating runtime panics and bounding peak memory to `O(tile + halo)` regardless of chromosome length or sequencing depth. In `v{{m:v110_study_version}}`, `VarDictRust` pairs this default parity baseline with `{{m:v110_compat_switches}}` opt-in accuracy, SV/mate-pileup, somatic multi-allelic/amplicon, VCFv4.2/v4.3 schema, and modern-aligner (`--eqx`) corrections behind `--compat=off`, alongside clinical `--preset` modes (`wgs`, `wes`, `amplicon`, `ctdna`), `--amplicon-single-primer` (`-sp`), and all `{{m:v110_var2vcf_flags}}/{{m:v110_var2vcf_flags}}` consolidated `var2vcf` post-filters including `--vcf-all-alleles` (native consolidation of `var2vcf_*.pl -A` [1]; `Table 9`, `Figure 9`, `Supplementary Table S7`).

We tested `VarDictRust`'s `O(threads × (tile + halo))` memory bound across four scaling benchmarks (`Table 4`, `Figure 4`, `Supplementary Table S3`):

## Discussion

### Scientific Software Consolidation Under the `rewrites.bio` Specification
Rewriting a clinical variant caller for speed risks changing Smith-Waterman tie-breaking, Phred quality offsets, floating-point rounding, or strand-bias p-values, which forces clinical laboratories to re-validate pipelines. Conversely, line-by-line transliteration preserves memory growth and crash bugs. `VarDictRust` separates **default oracle parity (`--compat=vardictjava`) and crash/memory robustness** from **opt-in algorithmic corrections (`--compat=off`)** under the [rewrites.bio](https://rewrites.bio) specification. In default mode (`v{{m:version}}` through `v{{m:current_release_version}}`), `VarDictRust` reproduces the exact allele calls, `FILTER` flags, somatic `STATUS` classifications, and `HALF_EVEN`-rounded Fisher exact statistics of `{{m:oracle_version}}` (`Table 1`, `Supplementary Table S2`), while eliminating JVM `OutOfMemoryError` failures, `CigarModifier` bounds panics (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`), and `java.util.HashMap` row-order non-determinism (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#282`). In `v{{m:v110_study_version}}`, opt-in `--compat=off` (`CompatProfile`, stamping `##VarDictRustCompat=off` without per-bug `--fix` CLI flag sprawl) activates `{{m:v110_compat_switches}}` workspace-tested corrections across five functional families (`Table 9`, `Supplementary Table S7`). The schema and SV families enforce VCFv4.2/v4.3 `FORMAT/AD` `Number=R` cardinality, non-SV `INFO/END` omission, IUPAC/`SHIFT3` FASTA concordance, canonical `0/1` diploid genotypes, 1-bp symbolic `<DEL>`/`<DUP>`/`<INV>` anchoring, signed `SVLEN`, `-L` length bounds, and lazy mate-window pileups. The calling, alignment, and accounting families add multi-allelic somatic continuation (`joachimwolff/VarDictJava#4`), paired tumor-normal amplicon calling (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#378`), directional `--adaptor` soft-clip screening (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#240`), outer hard-clip stripping, homopolymer CIGAR coalescing, `--eqx` (`=`/`X`) read-length accounting, `-p` pileup coordinate retention, and `VD <= DP <= RD + VD` depth/strand/IEEE-754 `HALF_EVEN`/coordinate-sorted reconciliation—paired with `--preset` (`wgs`, `wes`, `amplicon`, `ctdna`), `--vcf-all-alleles`, and `--amplicon-single-primer` (`-sp`) [27]. Default mode (`--compat=vardictjava` / `--parity`) preserves upstream `VarDictJava` output quirks across `v1.x`, while specification-gated `NM` over-filtering (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#62`), realignment-drop (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#363`), `<1%` low-AF noise (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#381`), and indel/delins boundary-normalization (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#264`) heuristics remain deferred to `v1.1.1+`, and native UMI/duplex consensus (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#374`), `F1R2`/`F2R1` orientation-bias modeling, and `--preset hifi|ont` profiles to `v2.0` (`Table 8`, `Supplementary Note 3`).

### Impact of Bounded Streaming on T2T-Q100 and CMRG Discovery
Whole-interval heap allocation in `VarDictJava` and `vardictcpp` has historically restricted whole-genome `VarDict` calling to pre-chunked `≤{{m:giab_arm_b_max_kbp}}` kbp BED masks (`GIAB v4.2.1`) or external `{{m:cpp_chunk_kbp}}` kbp BED-splitting scripts (`Table 5`). Keeping intervals $\le {{m:s1_tile_1m}}$ bp untiled and partitioning spans $> {{m:s1_tile_1m}}$ bp into `{{m:rust_default_tile_kbp}}` kbp tiles with `{{m:rust_halo_kbp}}` kbp realignment halos (`Table 1`) allows `VarDictRust` to call unchunked full chromosomes in `{{m:s5_hg002_contigs_rust_rss_gb}}`–`{{m:s5_hg008_contigs_rust_rss_gb}}` GB RAM. Removing the `50` kbp BED mask recovers `+{{m:s6_v50q_gain_gbp}}` Gbp of segmental duplications and complex loci in `NIST GIAB v5.0q T2T-Q100` (`+{{m:s6_v50q_snv_r_gain_pct}}` SNV recall, `+{{m:s6_v50q_indel_r_gain_pct}}` indel recall) and raises recall across the `273` `NIST CMRG v1.00` genes (`+{{m:s6_cmrg_snv_r_gain_pct}}` SNV, `+{{m:s6_cmrg_indel_r_gain_pct}}` indel; `Table 7`).

### Trade-Offs Against Deep-Learning, Commercial, and C++ Callers
On `364`× `HG008 v0.4` somatic WGS (`Table 7`, `Figure 7c`), deep-learning (`DeepSomatic`, `ClairS`, `Ultima DeepVariant Somatic`) and graph/haplotype (`DRAGEN`, `Lancet2`, `Hartwig SAGE`, `Mutect2`, `Strelka2`) callers achieve higher SNV precision in repetitive sequence. `VarDictRust`, however, achieves higher somatic indel recall (`{{m:s6_HG008_FullWGS_v04_All_VarDictRust_v106_indel_r_pct}}`) than `ClairS`, `DeepSomatic`, `Lancet2`, and `Ultima DeepVariant Somatic`. It retains `{{m:s6_titration_0p5vaf_recall_ratio_vs_m2}}`× higher SNV recall than `Mutect2` and `Strelka2` at `{{m:s6_tit_vaf_1_pct}}` VAF (`1%` purity; `Supplementary Table S4`) on commodity CPUs without GPUs or FPGAs. On pre-chunked `{{m:s2_fig1_fig3_wgs_bed_intervals}}`-interval panels (`SRR8657348`, `8` threads), `vardictcpp` finishes raw `.var` calling in `{{m:s2_SRR8657348_th8_cpp_wall_s}}` s vs `{{m:s2_SRR8657348_th8_rust_wall_s}}` s for `VarDictRust`. That gap reflects `vardictcpp` hardcoding `--nosv` and skipping `##contig` and `INFO/END` VCF validation (`Table 8`). `VarDictRust` instead completes every unchunked chromosome run and all `{{m:s5_chr20_total_runs}}` deep chromosome 20 runs with full SV and VCF output.

### Operational Boundary Conditions
Four settings govern production deployment:

1. **Multi-Megabase Single-Region Calling (`--tile-size` vs. `{{m:rust_default_tile_kbp}}` kbp Auto-Tiling):** Intervals $\le {{m:s1_tile_1m}}$ bp run untiled by default. On unchunked $> {{m:s1_tile_1m}}$ bp regions, default `{{m:rust_default_tile_kbp}}` kbp auto-tiling bounds `-R chr1:1-249250621` memory to `{{m:s2_rchr1_rust_rss_mb}}` MB (`Table 2`). It also prevents `VarDictJava`'s cross-centromere `36`–`81` Mbp SV pairing and `VD = 74 > DP` state leak (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#92`). Passing `--tile-size` equal to the region span reproduces `VarDictJava`'s single-window output byte-for-byte. Use `--tile-size 10000000` on `10` Mbp `chr1` `-f {{m:f_zero}}`. Use `--tile-size 250000000` on `{{m:s2_rchr1_mbp}}` Mbp `-R chr1` (`{{m:sw_span_f0_rows}}` `.var` / `{{m:s4_span_f0_vars}}` `.vcf` and `{{m:sw_rchr1_rows}}` `.var` rows; `Table 1`, `Supplementary Table S2`).

2. **`QUAL` Filtering on Standard-Depth Germline WGS:** Because `-f {{m:f_std}}` targets low-VAF somatic discovery, `35`×–`60`× germline WGS pipelines need filtering. Filter at `QUAL >= {{m:s4_t3_hg002_best_qual}}` ($F_1 = {{m:s4_t3_hg002_best_f1}}$ vs `{{m:s4_t3_hg002_none_f1}}` unfiltered, removing `{{m:s4_t3_hg002_fp_reduction_pct}}` of false positives; `Table 3`, `Supplementary Table S4`). Alternatively set `-f` to `0.10`–`0.20`.

3. **Dual-Mode `--compat=vardictjava|off` Policy:** Default invocations across `v1.x` (`--compat=vardictjava` / `--parity`) preserve upstream `VarDictJava` v1.8.3 output quirks (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#62`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#92`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#116`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#292`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#342`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#369`, `AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#398`) for zero-diff drop-in migration. In `v{{m:v110_study_version}}`, opt-in `--compat=off` stamps `##VarDictRustCompat=off` in VCF headers and activates `{{m:v110_compat_switches}}` `CompatProfile` fixes (`Supplementary Table S7`), while `--preset` (`wgs`, `wes`, `amplicon`, `ctdna`; `##VarDictRustPreset=<PRESET>`) configures short-read clinical workflows, `--vcf-all-alleles` (`--all-alleles`) enables multi-allelic VCF emission, and `--amplicon-single-primer` (`-sp`) supports single-primer extension panels (`Table 9`).

4. **Short- and Medium-Read Chemistry Scope vs. Long-Read Sequencing:** `Table 6` evaluates `{{m:s5_cross_platform_arms}}` short- and medium-read sequencing-by-synthesis, sequencing-by-binding (`PacBio Onso`, `~150` bp paired-end short reads; cf. external NIST `PacBio HiFi` `DeepSomatic` and `ClairS` callsets in `Table 7`), and flow-based chemistries rather than `PacBio HiFi` or `Oxford Nanopore` (`ONT`) long reads. Upstream `VarDictJava` v1.8.3 omits `=` and `X` sequence-match/mismatch CIGAR operators (emitted by `minimap2 --eqx` and `pbmm2`) from `CigarParser` read-length accumulation (`rlen1 = 0`, yielding negative `PMEAN` and dropping calls on `=`/`X` alignments), enforces short-read per-read mismatch (`-m {{m:default_max_mm}}`), `var2vcf` mean-mismatch (`NM`), and indel-size (`-I {{m:default_indel_size_bp}}` bp) defaults that even `15`–`20` kbp `M`-operator alignments exceed, and does not chain split-read `SA:Z` supplementary alignments across multi-kilobase reads. Benchmarking unmodified strict-parity presets on `15`–`20` kbp `HiFi` or `ONT` alignments would therefore conflate upstream short-read algorithmic parameterization with engine implementation quality. In `VarDictRust` `v{{m:current_release_version}}`, native `=`/`X` (`--eqx`) CIGAR read-length and coalescing support (`eqx_cigar_read_length`) ships alongside all `{{m:v110_compat_switches}}` `CompatProfile` corrections under opt-in `--compat=off` (`Table 9`, `Supplementary Table S7`), while default `--compat=vardictjava` (`--parity`, certified fingerprint `{{m:current_release_fingerprint}}`) preserves strict `VarDictJava v1.8.3` parity; short-read clinical presets (`--preset` across `wgs`, `wes`, `amplicon`, and `ctdna`) ship in `v{{m:current_release_version}}`, whereas dedicated `--preset hifi|ont` long-read profiles, native UMI/duplex consensus (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#374`), and `F1R2`/`F2R1` orientation-bias modeling remain slated for `v2.0`.

## Conclusions

`VarDictRust` (`v{{m:current_release_version}}`; `{{m:total_cells}}`-experiment cloud benchmark archived at `v{{m:version}}`) consolidates the `VarDictJava -> R -> Perl` pipeline into a single static Rust binary. It holds exact Tier-1/2 oracle parity (`ε = {{m:eps_tol}}`, `HALF_EVEN`, record-aligned) across `{{m:s2_modes_total}}` operational modes, `{{m:s5_cross_platform_arms}}` sequencing chemistries (parity on all completing paired runs), and `{{m:s6_ga4gh_cells}}` completed Rust GA4GH `v3.5` truth evaluations. Bounding memory to `O(tile + halo)` delivers `{{m:s2_rchr1_speedup_vs_java}}`× faster chromosome-scale calling (`{{m:s2_rchr1_rss_ratio_vs_java}}`× lower RSS) and `{{m:s4_exome_th32_speedup_vs_java}}`× faster `32`-thread whole-exome calling (`{{m:s4_exome_th32_rss_ratio_vs_java}}`× lower RSS), unlocking unchunked whole-genome discovery across `GIAB v5.0q T2T-Q100` and `CMRG v1.00`, while opt-in `v{{m:v110_study_version}}` `--compat=off` and `--preset` modes resolve targeted VCFv4.2/v4.3 schema/canonicalization defects (`{{m:v110_bcftools_norm_off}}` `bcftools norm` pass) and rescue subclonal diploid somatic calls (`Table 9`, `Figure 9`).

## Methods

### Consolidated Rust Architecture and Coordinate Type Safety
`VarDictRust` (certified release `v{{m:current_release_version}}`, providing default `--compat=vardictjava` parity alongside opt-in `--compat=off`, `--preset` across `wgs`, `wes`, `amplicon`, and `ctdna`, `-sp`, and `--vcf-all-alleles`) is a two-crate Rust workspace: `vardictrust-core` (library engine, compiled with `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` and zero `unwrap()`/`expect()`/`panic!()` calls outside unit tests) and `vardictrust` (`clap` CLI exposing `{{m:cli_flag_count}}` flags across `{{m:cli_group_count}}` functional groups, including all `{{m:v110_var2vcf_flags}}` consolidated `var2vcf` post-filters, `--vcf-all-alleles`, `--preset`, `--compat=vardictjava|off`, multithreaded BGZF `--output` streaming, and native `--tbi`/`--csi` indexing). Distinct `Pos0` (`0`-based half-open BAM/CRAM coordinates from `noodles`) and `Pos1` (`1`-based inclusive VCF/VarDict coordinates) newtypes enforce explicit `.to_one_based()` and `.to_zero_based()` conversions. Sequences and qualities are processed as byte slices (`&[u8]`) with Phred+33 saturation arithmetic (`Phred(byte.saturating_sub(33))`). Continuous verification spans `{{m:workspace_test_count}}` automated Cargo workspace tests (`{{m:core_lib_test_count}}` `vardictrust-core` unit tests), `{{m:harness_self_test_count}}` harness diagnostic self-tests, and in-process `cargo-fuzz` hostile-input harnesses.

### Bounded Tile-Plus-Halo Streaming and Deterministic Realignment
Intervals $\le {{m:s1_tile_1m}}$ bp execute as a single untiled window. Intervals $> {{m:s1_tile_1m}}$ bp (or with explicit `--tile-size`) are partitioned into non-overlapping ownership tiles $[T_k^{\text{start} }, T_k^{\text{end} })$ of default width `{{m:rust_default_tile_kbp}}` kbp. Each tile is padded on both sides by a `{{m:rust_halo_kbp}}` kbp realignment halo (`{{m:rust_splice_halo_kbp}}` kbp in `-i` splice mode; `Figure 2b`, `Figure 4d`). A `Rayon` work-stealing pool streams alignments overlapping $[T_k^{\text{start} } - H, T_k^{\text{end} } + H)$ via `noodles` (multi-threaded BGZF BAM and reference-decoded CRAM `-G`). It runs CIGAR modification (`cigar/modify.rs`), pileup accumulation (`cigar/parse.rs`), local realignment (`realign.rs`), SV assembly (`sv.rs`), and variant finalization (`tovars.rs`). It emits only variants whose canonical `Pos1` start falls in $[T_k^{\text{start} }, T_k^{\text{end} })$. Per-tile pileup maps drop immediately upon tile completion (RAII), whole-stream VCF candidate accumulation spills to disk above `{{m:vcf_spill_cap_mib}}` MiB (`SpillableVcfAccumulator`), and indexed BAM/CRAM decoding pairs pre-validated `.bai`/`.csi` structural and size bounds (`{{m:max_bai_cap_mib}}` MiB `.bai`; `{{m:max_csi_cap_mib}}` MiB compressed/decompressed `.csi` caps) with singleflight CRAM container caching (`{{m:cram_container_cap_mib}}` MiB per container across a `{{m:cram_total_cache_mib}}` MiB `{{m:cram_cache_shards}}`-shard LRU cache), a `{{m:fasta_cache_cap_mib}}` MiB shared FASTA reference cache, two-slot paired somatic `CRAM_META_TLS` state, and `SeekWindowReader` slice validation. Where `VarDictJava` output order depends on `java.util.HashMap` bucket iteration (`AstraZeneca-NGS/VarDictJava#282`), `VarDictRust` ports OpenJDK 8 `HashMap` spread, bin, resize, and `TreeNode` Red-Black treeification semantics in `JavaHashMap`, and uses `IndexMap` (`LinkedHashMap`) and `BTreeMap` (`TreeMap`) elsewhere.

### In-Process Statistical Post-Processing and Banker's Rounding (`HALF_EVEN`)
`VarDictRust` computes hypergeometric two-sided Fisher exact p-values and conditional maximum-likelihood odds ratios (`uniroot` zero-in with tolerance $\text{ulp}(1)^{0.25}$) natively in Rust (`stats/fisher.rs`), followed by VCF 4.2 (`VCFv4.2`) formatting (`output/`), replacing R (`teststrandbias.R` / `testsomatic.R`) and Perl (`var2vcf_valid.pl` / `var2vcf_paired.pl`). Default mode uses a cleanroom saddlepoint hypergeometric kernel (`stats/hypergeom.rs`, Catherine Loader 2000 binomial deviance and Stirling error with extended-precision `f80_sum` accumulation), whereas `--fisher` replicates `VarDictJava`'s Apache Commons Math 3 `SaddlePointExpansion` / `HypergeometricDistribution` path; hypergeometric supports exceeding `{{m:max_dense_fisher_mib}}` MiB ($2^{24}$ points) switch from dense `logdc` tables to unimodal `ln P(j) - ln P(mode) >= -750` active-window summation. Floating-point output fields apply IEEE 754 round-half-to-even (`round_half_even`) at exact upstream field precisions—`5` decimal places for `SBF`, `ODDRATIO`, and `SSF`; `4` decimal places for `AF`, `AdjAF`, and `HIAF`; `3` decimal places for `MSI` and `SN`; and `1` decimal place for `QMEAN`, `PMEAN`, `MQ`, and `NM` feeding integer `QUAL`—matching R `round()` and Java `DecimalFormat` (`HALF_EVEN`) in both no-`--fisher` (trailing zeros kept) and `--fisher` (trailing zeros stripped) modes within `ε = {{m:eps_tol}}` (${{m:eps_sci}}$).

### Cloud Benchmarking, Reproducibility Protocol, and Statistical Analysis
All `{{m:total_cells}}` cloud benchmark cells ran on Google Cloud Platform (`{{m:host}}`, `32` vCPUs, `128` GiB RAM, with `-Xmx200g` full-WGS comparator baselines provisioned on `n2-highmem-32` `256` GiB RAM nodes sharing identical Intel Xeon Platinum 8373C Ice Lake silicon; `{{m:nvme_raid0_tb}}` TB local NVMe RAID0 scratch; pinned to `VarDictRust` `v{{m:version}}`, fingerprint `{{m:fingerprint}}`, exact commands and SHA-256 manifests in `Supplementary Methods` and `Supplementary Table S1`); subsequent releases through `v{{m:current_release_version}}` (`fingerprint` `{{m:current_release_fingerprint}}`) preserve zero-diff default oracle equivalence across all certified cohorts. Timed cells ran `n = 3` independent technical replicates under `/usr/bin/time -v` to record wall-clock seconds and `Maximum resident set size (kbytes)`. Wall-clock timeouts surface as exit code `rc=124` (`3`-hour limit); JVM heap exhaustion surfaces as `rc=3` (`OutOfMemoryError`). Binary concordance proportions use exact two-sided Clopper-Pearson `95%` binomial CIs ($\alpha = 0.05$); see `Supplementary Note 3` for the Beta-inverse derivation and `--compat=vardictjava|off` release governance. Runtime and RSS across the `12` AML panel configurations were compared using two-sided Mann-Whitney $U$ tests and two-sided exact paired sign-flip permutation tests ($2^{-{{m:s2_tierp_sign_perm_exp_twosided}}} = {{m:s2_tierp_sign_perm_p_twosided}}$; one-sided $2^{-12} = {{m:s2_tierp_sign_perm_p}}$). Cloud truth concordance (`Tables 3–7`) was evaluated with `rtg vcfeval` (`v3.12.1`, allele-presence `--squash-ploidy` on somatic cohorts) against official NIST GIAB and SEQC2 high-confidence BED masks (`HG002 v4.2.1`, `HG002 v5.0q T2T-Q100`, `CMRG v1.00`, `Mosaic v1.10`, `HG005 v4.2.1`, `SEQC2 v1.2.1`, and `HG008 v0.3`/`v0.4`), whereas `Table 9` Domain 2 evaluated ploidy-strict diploid `rtg vcfeval` without `--squash-ploidy`. *In silico* tumor-purity titrations (`20%`, `10%`, `5%`, `2%`, `1%`) were built via seed-controlled `samtools view -s` subsampling and merging of HG008 tumor and normal BAMs (`Supplementary Methods`).

The `v{{m:v110_study_version}}` opt-in `--compat=off` and `--preset` evaluation (`Figure 9`, `Table 9`, `Supplementary Table S7`; `benchmarks/v110-compat-study/`, pinned to `VarDictRust` `v{{m:v110_study_version}}`, source fingerprint `{{m:v110_study_fingerprint}}`, performance fingerprint `{{m:v110_study_perf_fingerprint}}`) executes `{{m:v110_total_arms}}` host-independent deterministic callset, targeted `CompatProfile` VCF schema/canonicalization/accounting, FASTA reference-concordance (`bcftools norm --check-ref e`, `bcftools` `v{{m:v110_bcftools_version}}`, `samtools` `v{{m:v110_samtools_version}}`, `tabix` `v{{m:v110_tabix_version}}`) [26], `QIAseq V3` single-primer extension (`ENA SRP295113`) [27], and diploid `rtg vcfeval` (`rtg-tools` `v{{m:v110_rtg_version}}`, invoked with `--all-records --vcf-score-field=QUAL` and without `--squash-ploidy`) [12] verification domains via `paper/collect_v110_compat_metrics.py`, recording zero host-dependent wall-clock or RSS measurements so single-host cloud timing isolation is strictly preserved (`Supplementary Methods 1.8`). Clopper-Pearson `95%` CIs in `Table 9` and `Results` are computed strictly over independent biological truth-set variant denominators (`SEQC2` somatic truth variants, `GIAB HG008 v0.4` somatic truth variants, and `QIAseq` `KnownPositives`), never over correlated per-callset VCF schema rows.

```bash
vardictrust -G GRCh38.fa -f {{m:f_std}} -N "TUMOR|NORMAL" \

```
