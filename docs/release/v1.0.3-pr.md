## Key Additions & Improvements in `v1.0.3` (incorporating `v1.0.2` – `v1.0.3`)

### 1. Deep-Pileup, Allocator & High-Region Exome Optimizations (`PR #35`, `PR #37`)
- **Zero-Allocation Hot-Path Pileup & Intrusive `JavaHashMap` (`O-1`–`O-12a`, `PR #35`):**
  - Replaced per-bucket `Vec` allocations in `JavaHashMap` with a flat intrusive node arena (`buckets: Vec<u32>`, `nodes: Vec<Node<K, V>>`, free-list reuse), preserving exact OpenJDK 8+ bucket/chain iteration and `treeify_bin` semantics.
  - Eliminated heap allocations in `get_variation` / `get_variation_maybe` (`IndexMap` `raw_entry_mut_v1` with stack UTF-8 keys), `round_half_even` (`StackBuf<64>`), `SeedMap` (`SeedKey` 17-byte inline key), `ToVarsBuilder` (sparse copy-on-write `FxHashMap` overlay), and `CigarModifier::modify_cigar` (dynamic `(has_d, has_i, has_s)` pre-filters + byte-slice post-loop matchers).
  - Enabled `noodles-bgzf/libdeflate` and configured `tikv-jemallocator` with static `_rjem_malloc_conf` (`background_thread:true,dirty_decay_ms:250,muzzy_decay_ms:0,narenas:8`), cutting peak RSS by **1.7×–4.2×** (`hg002` `497.1 MiB`, `seqc2` `467.9 MiB`, `hg008` `313.1 MiB`, `chr1` 249 Mbp `1.42 GiB`).
- **Exome BED Multi-Tile Batching & BGZF Block Caching (`EBO-1..EBO-3`, `PR #37`):**
  - Added `CachedBgzfReader` with a 1-block thread-local cursor and 8-shard × 16-slot LRU process-wide `BGZF_BLOCK_CACHE` (`EBO-1`), lazy `OnceCell<RawSeedMap>` reference k-mer seeding (`EBO-2`), and 16-tile same-chromosome batching in `regions::batch_tiles` / `runner::run_parallel_batched` (`EBO-3`).
  - Reduced `seqc2-exome-tier2` wall time by **2.51×** (`19m 28.78s` $\to$ `7m 46.34s`, **6.9× faster** than VarDictJava) and `scale.json` exome BED wall time across 1–8 threads by **1.38×–2.38×** (`3m 06.43s` at `-th 8`).
